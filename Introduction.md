Introduction :

This case study looks at one of the biggest changes of not only how e-commerce changes the way retail businesses operates but also how it affected billions of people worldwide. online e-commerce, though shaped decades earlier, but seen through and getting more popularised with Amazon and Ebay. Started as an online bookstore and an auction site, both grew as a global international marketplaces. this transformation affected how people buy,selland get goods and services.

we will discuss these changes from 2 viewpoints : Societal and Business. From the business pov, it changed how traditional brick and mortar shops operates , how merchants can be committed to fully sell online.From a societal pov, it changed people perception about shopping.

We also examine the ethical issues it raises, especially around data privacy, working conditions, the decline of small local retailers, and the digital divide, and we suggest areas for improvement.
This development matters because it shows the wider shift from traditional brick and mortar retail shops to digital commerce. It demonstrates how online platforms can reshape both how businesses operate and how people behave socially.
